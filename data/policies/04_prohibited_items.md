# Prohibited and Restricted Items

## Prohibited (cannot be shipped under any circumstances)
- Firearms, ammunition, and explosives.
- Illegal drugs and controlled substances.
- Flammable liquids and compressed gas cylinders.
- Live animals.
- Counterfeit goods.

## Restricted (requires special handling and pre-approval)
- Lithium battery devices above 100Wh (large power banks, e-bike batteries).
- Perishable food items (require cold-chain warehouses only).
- High-value jewellery above LKR 500,000 (requires insured shipping and signature
  on delivery).
- Medical items requiring refrigeration.

## Fragile Items (require fragile packaging, not prohibited)
Glassware, electronics, mirrors, ceramics, refrigerators, televisions, and musical
instruments must be packed with fragile-rated packaging materials. See the Damage
Claims Policy for how fault is determined if this rule is not followed.

## Customer Responsibility
Customers must accurately declare the item type when creating an order. Misdeclared
prohibited items may result in the order being cancelled without refund.
