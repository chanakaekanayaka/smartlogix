# Damage Claims Policy

## Reporting a Damaged Item
Customers must report a damaged item within 48 hours of delivery, with photos where
possible, and their order ID.

## Fault Determination
SmartLogix determines fault by reviewing:
- The warehouse packaging record (was the item labeled "Fragile" if it required
  fragile handling?).
- The courier's handling history (past damage incidents for that courier).
- The condition noted at pickup versus the condition reported at delivery.

If the item required fragile packaging and the warehouse record shows no fragile
label was applied, the claim is treated as a **warehouse packaging fault** and a
full refund plus free replacement (where stock allows) is offered.

If the warehouse packaging record is correct but the courier has a history of
damage incidents on the same route, the claim is treated as a **courier handling
fault** and a full refund is offered.

If neither warehouse nor courier records show an error, the claim is marked
**unclear** and escalated to a human support agent for manual review rather than
an automatic decision.

## Fragile Item Categories
The following categories always require fragile packaging: glassware, electronics,
mirrors, ceramics, refrigerators, televisions, and musical instruments.

## Compensation for Damaged Items
- Warehouse or courier fault: full refund + free replacement if item is in stock,
  otherwise full refund only.
- Unclear fault: escalated to human review, temporary partial refund (25%) offered
  while investigation continues.
