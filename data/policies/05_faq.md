# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Provide your order ID to the SmartLogix assistant and it will look up the latest
status from the courier and warehouse records.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**
A: Address changes are allowed only if the order has not yet left the warehouse.
Once marked "In Transit", the address cannot be changed for that delivery.

**Q: What happens if I'm not home for delivery?**
A: The courier will attempt delivery twice. After two failed attempts, the parcel
is returned to the nearest warehouse and the customer is charged a redelivery fee.

**Q: Is my personal data shared with couriers?**
A: Only the minimum information needed to complete delivery (name, phone number,
address) is shared with the assigned courier. It is never sold or shared with
third parties for marketing.

**Q: Can I delete my data from SmartLogix?**
A: Yes, customers can request account and order history deletion at any time,
subject to Sri Lankan legal record-keeping requirements for completed transactions.

**Q: How accurate are the delivery cost and time estimates?**
A: Estimates are calculated from distance, item weight, and current warehouse load.
They are approximate and may vary slightly from the final invoice.
