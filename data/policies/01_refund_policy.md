# SmartLogix Refund Policy

SmartLogix partners with courier and warehouse operators across Sri Lanka to ship customer
orders. This policy explains when a customer is entitled to a refund.

## Full Refund
A full refund is issued when:
- The parcel is confirmed lost in transit and cannot be located within 7 working days.
- The parcel arrives damaged and the damage is traced to a warehouse packaging error
  (for example, a fragile item shipped without fragile packaging).
- The parcel is delivered to the wrong address due to a courier or warehouse error.

## Partial Refund (50%)
A partial refund of 50% of the delivery fee is issued when:
- Delivery is delayed by more than 48 hours beyond the promised delivery window due to
  operational issues (courier scheduling, vehicle breakdown).
- The item arrives with minor cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality.

## No Refund
No refund is issued when:
- The delay or damage is caused by severe weather, natural disasters, or other events
  outside SmartLogix's control (force majeure).
- The customer provided an incorrect or incomplete delivery address.
- The customer was unavailable to receive the delivery after two attempted deliveries.

## Processing Time
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 working days to the customer's original
payment method.

## How to Claim
Customers can request a refund through the SmartLogix assistant by describing the
issue and providing their order ID. All refund decisions include a clear explanation
of how the decision was reached.
