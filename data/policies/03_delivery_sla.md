# Delivery SLA (Service Level Agreement)

## Standard Delivery Windows (within Sri Lanka)
- Same city (e.g. Colombo to Colombo): 1 working day.
- Nearby cities (under 100 km, e.g. Colombo to Kandy): 1-2 working days.
- Distant cities (100-250 km, e.g. Colombo to Jaffna): 2-4 working days.
- Remote areas (over 250 km or limited courier coverage): 3-5 working days.

## Delay Thresholds
- A delivery is considered "on time" if it arrives within the promised window.
- A delivery delayed by up to 24 hours beyond the window is "minor delay" - no
  compensation, but the customer is notified proactively.
- A delivery delayed by more than 48 hours is "major delay" - eligible for a 50%
  delivery fee refund per the Refund Policy.

## Weather and Force Majeure
Delays caused by declared severe weather warnings, flooding, or civil disruption
are excluded from SLA penalties, but the customer is still notified with a revised
delivery estimate.

## Tracking
Every order can be tracked using its order ID. Status values are: Processing,
In Transit, Delivered, Delayed, Damaged, or Lost.
